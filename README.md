### *Conclusion & Recommendations*

*Slide 14-15: Executive Summary & Path to Financial Health*
(This is where you impress them with the analysis we just did)

"Based on this data, we have developed a *'Path to Financial Health'* for Nithin.
We identified that roughly *47%* of his spending falls into 'Wants' rather than 'Needs.'
Our top recommendation is to address what we call the *'Snack Trap.'* Nithin spent over *₹5,000 on snacks* alone—almost as much as he spent on basic food grains. By cutting snack spending in half and reducing dining out, he could save an additional *₹2,000 per month*."
